# CMS and listing models

## Global settings

- Company name
- Established year
- Tagline
- WhatsApp and telephone
- Email and domain
- Address/appointment wording
- Opening hours
- Social links
- Navigation and footer links
- Regulatory details
- Default SEO/share image
- Site-wide alert
- Last verified date for business information

## Standard page

- Internal name
- Title and slug
- Summary
- Flexible approved sections
- Images/video with alt text and consent status
- Primary/secondary CTA
- FAQs
- SEO title/description
- Social image
- Draft/published state
- Last reviewed date

## Blog article

- Title and slug
- Excerpt
- Cover image and alt text
- Author, defaulting to “Red Brick Lettings”
- Category and tags
- Article body
- Published and reviewed dates
- Source list
- Related articles
- Social caption and share image
- SEO fields
- Legal/factual review flag

Categories: Landlords, Tenants, Peterborough, Maintenance, Property Guidance.

## Property listing

Public-safe fields:

- Reference, slug, status and featured flag
- Title, summary and approved description
- Public area and outward postcode
- Approximate public coordinates and exact-location permission
- Rent PCM, deposit, holding deposit and known bills
- Availability date and furnishing
- Property type, bedrooms, bathrooms, receptions and size
- Verified features
- EPC, council tax and material-information fields
- Gallery, floor plans, video/virtual-tour link and alt text
- Enquiry reference
- SEO/share fields
- Published, reviewed and expiry dates

Restricted fields must be access-controlled and never sent to the public page payload:

- Full address/postcode where not approved for publication
- Entry/access notes
- Alarm, key or security information
- Landlord or tenant information
- Internal maintenance or management notes

## Rental report

- Submitted property characteristics
- Geography and data observation date
- Source/retrieval date/licence/quality per evidence item
- Indicative low/high monthly rent
- Comparable count and confidence explanation
- Separate rent, sold-price and scenario series
- Licensing-area caution flag
- Neutral crime context and disclaimer
- Amenities/transport context
- Property considerations and opportunities
- One-/three-year low, central and high scenarios
- Disclaimer and human-review state
- Lead/contact consent kept separately from analytics data

## Maintenance

- Landlord/tenant route
- Repair categories
- Urgency guidance
- Reporter details and property reference
- Description and uploads
- Permission/contact preference
- Submission destination
- Privacy wording
- Confirmation/failure message

Do not store emergency/security details in analytics or sample content.
