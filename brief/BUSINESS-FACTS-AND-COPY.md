## Words and claims to avoid

Do not publish:

- “Best”, “leading”, “excellent”, “cheapest” or “most affordable”
- “Guaranteed rent”, “guaranteed tenant” or guaranteed outcome
- 24/7, emergency attendance or fixed response times
- A current property count
- Reviews or testimonials that have not been supplied with permission
- Awards, affiliations, schemes, redress membership or regulatory credentials not verified
- National coverage or a walk-in Peterborough office
- A specific email address, domain or opening hours until confirmed

## Tone rules

- UK spelling
- Plain English
- Short, useful headings
- Direct but never harsh
- No slang, exaggerated friendliness or fear-based selling
- Explain legal/market information carefully and link to sources
- Treat landlords and tenants with equal respect
