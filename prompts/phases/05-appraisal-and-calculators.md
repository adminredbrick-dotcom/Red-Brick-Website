# Phase 5 — appraisal and useful calculators

Build the indicative rental-estimate journey and tenant move-in cost calculator through typed, replaceable data adapters.

Keep rental evidence, completed-sale context and illustrative future scenarios visually and semantically separate. Every result model must support source, geography, observation date, retrieval date, quality and confidence. Use honest unavailable and weak-evidence states rather than manufactured values.

Crime information is neutral context only. Never create a safe/unsafe or good/bad area score. Do not use demographics or benefit receipt in pricing or tenant-selection logic.

Done when:

- Forms and calculations have unit tests and accessible validation.
- Low-evidence, unavailable-data and adapter-error states work.
- Scenarios are labelled illustrative rather than forecasts/guarantees.
- The landlord result ends with “Request a verified rental appraisal.”
- No result presents demo data as current market evidence.

Report evidence and stop before Phase 6.
