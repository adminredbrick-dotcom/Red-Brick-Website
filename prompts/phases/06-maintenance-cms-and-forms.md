# Phase 6 — maintenance, CMS and forms

Build the maintenance experience, repair-report interface, landlord and tenant pages, Sanity-backed Insights, article template, contact flow and social metadata.

Follow the supplied content models and claim guardrails. Forms require accessible validation and truthful success/failure behaviour. If a real endpoint is absent, state that online submission is not active and provide the confirmed WhatsApp route. Never imply 24/7 support or a fixed response time.

Do not publish unconfirmed regulatory, contact, fee or service details. Keep contact/lead data separate from analytics and never log sensitive form bodies by default.

Done when:

- Red Brick can edit pages, listings and articles through documented CMS fields.
- Maintenance routes and urgent guidance are clear without unsupported promises.
- Form pending/success/error states have been verified.
- Article metadata, reviewed dates, sources and social previews work.
- Setup and content-editing instructions are current.

Report evidence and stop before Phase 7.
