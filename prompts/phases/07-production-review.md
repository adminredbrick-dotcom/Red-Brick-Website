# Phase 7 — independent production review

Audit and polish the entire project against `acceptance/DEFINITION-OF-DONE.md`. Use a fresh verifier/subagent where available; do not rely only on implementation notes.

Test 320, 375, 768, 1024 and 1440px; keyboard-only use; 200% zoom; reduced motion; slow loading; failed video; failed WebGL; absent map key; empty property results; adapter errors; and every form error/success state.

Inspect console/network output, broken links, metadata, sitemap, structured data, secrets exposure, indexing rules, privacy defaults, layout shift and performance.

Target Lighthouse:

- Accessibility 95+
- Best Practices 90+
- SEO 90+
- Mobile Performance 80+

Fix failures rather than simply reporting them. Do not call the build complete until the required evidence exists.

Final deliverables:

- Setup/deployment guide
- Content-editing guide
- Owner-decision and placeholder register
- Test and Lighthouse summary
- Desktop/mobile/reduced-motion screenshots
- Launch checklist separating ready items from legal/content/integration blockers
